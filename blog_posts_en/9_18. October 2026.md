# ESTimators Goodwood competition

In October 2026, the team "ESTimators" traveled to Chichester, England, for the international Greenpower F24+ competition. The race took place at the Goodwood Motor Circuit. For the journey, the team split into two groups: three members and a supervisor drove from Estonia to England with a vehicle and trailer, while the rest of the team traveled by plane. The drive took three full days, meaning those traveling by road set off on October 14th, whereas those flying were able to attend school until Friday. The group traveling by road stayed overnight in four different countries: Poland, Belgium, Germany, and, of course, England.
At the competition, we were granted one test run; however, we only managed to last half of the allotted hour, which signaled that we still had some final issues to iron out. After two hours spent searching for and fixing subtle, hidden technical glitches, it was time to cross our fingers and hope we could last the full hour during the main race.
One by one, other teams' cars began to fail, unable to manage their battery energy efficiently enough. Fortunately, we managed to complete the full hour-long race, securing 22nd place out of 29 in our category. Considering we were competing against the very best Greenpower cars—vehicles that had been under development for several years—we certainly cannot complain about our result. After the trip, we have big plans to switch categories—moving from "KIT" cars to "SCRATCH" cars. This will give us more freedom to build a vehicle that adheres to the regulations while allowing us to design it exactly how we want. At the end of the competition, we took the opportunity to get to know the other teams and examine their cars more closely; these generous engineers shared a wealth of knowledge and tips—both on what to do and what to avoid. Seeing the competition provided a great boost in motivation, but motivation alone isn't enough. ESTimators doesn't run on motivation—it runs on discipline. Watch out—ESTimators is picking up speed!

This event was only made possible thanks to our generous partners. Thank you Postimees, Tabasalu Karting Track, Komposiit Eesti, Espak, Noctua, and Fifaa!
