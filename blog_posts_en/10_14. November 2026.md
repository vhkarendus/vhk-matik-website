# PROTO – November 14–15

On Saturday, November 14, the ESTimators team headed to the PROTO Invention Factory with two of our cars to take part in the Great Inventors Festival. Our goal was to introduce young visitors to our vehicles and give them the opportunity to try out our realistic driving simulator.

Upon arriving on Saturday morning, we immediately got to work setting up our cars and making sure the simulator was comfortable for its users by placing soft fleece blankets inside the car body. Once the festival opened, we welcomed many young visitors who wanted to try our simulator. We also introduced our team and shared our future plans with those who showed a deeper interest in our work.
After spending the entire day talking to visitors, it was finally time to head home. Following a good night's rest, the team returned to PROTO the next morning with plenty of plans for the day.
This time, we were in the spotlight and had the opportunity to introduce our team to visitors in more detail. At around 2 PM, it was time for ESTimators to present itself in front of a larger audience. After the presentation, it was time to put theory into practice. We headed outside, behind the building, where we demonstrated our car's actual speed and manoeuvrability to the interested visitors.
We continued offering the simulator to the remaining young visitors until the festival came to an end. After a long and eventful weekend, it was finally time to head home.
Thank you, Proto!
